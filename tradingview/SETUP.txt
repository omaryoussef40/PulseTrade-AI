OPENING GAP + FIRST 15-MINUTE VOLUME

Designed for US stocks with a 09:30 America/New_York regular-session open.

RULES
Bullish: 09:30 open strictly above the previous trading day's regular-session high.
Bearish: 09:30 open strictly below the previous trading day's regular-session low.
Both: completed 09:30–09:45 volume >= 1.5 times the arithmetic mean of the
same opening interval over the previous 20 trading sessions, excluding today.
An open exactly at PDH/PDL does not qualify. A later breakout after opening
inside yesterday's range does not qualify. There is no score or follow-through
filter and no requirement to remain beyond PDH/PDL after opening.

INSTALL
1. Open TradingView Supercharts, then Pine Editor.
2. Paste opening_gap_volume_scanner.pine into a new indicator and save.
3. Use a standard daily (1D) chart to add/check it, and favorite the indicator.
4. Open Products > Screeners > Pine.
5. Select the watchlist or index you want to scan and choose this indicator.
6. Set the study timeframe to 1D. This is intentional: it reads the 15-minute
   opening candle internally and computes its average on daily history.
7. Keep the inputs at 1.5 and 20.
8. Bullish view: filter 'Above PDH match' equal to 1.
9. Bearish view: remove the bullish filter and filter 'Below PDL match' equal
   to 1. Do not require both filters simultaneously; they are mutually exclusive.
10. Sort by 'Opening volume ratio' descending. Rescan after 09:45 New York,
    allowing for your data feed's delay. Save two views if your UI supports it,
    or switch between these two filters on the same indicator.

SCOPE AND DATA
Pine Screener scans one selected watchlist or index at a time; this file does
not populate a market-wide stock universe. Cover your desired exchanges by
supplying suitable watchlists or scanning multiple index sources in sequence.
The script uses two request calls and ten output plots.
The daily screener timeframe avoids relying on just 500 fifteen-minute chart
bars for a 20-session baseline. Intrabar data must still be available from
your TradingView feed/plan. Missing opening candles or fewer than 20 valid
prior sessions produce Data ready = 0, not an approximate average.
Today-only filtering suppresses old-session matches on holidays or suspended
symbols. Before 09:45 there are no confirmed matches. Matches remain for the
session even if the stock subsequently reverses. Disable today-only on a daily
chart to inspect historical signals. No orders are placed by this indicator.
